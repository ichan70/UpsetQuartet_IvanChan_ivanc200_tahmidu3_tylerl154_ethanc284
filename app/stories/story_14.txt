Title: Satire Story Example
Author: UpsetQuartet
Created At: 2026-11-17 23:37:57

It was the best of times. There was plenty of grain to go around, to feed to populace. It was the worst of times, with the grain being coarse and filthy; it was said that dogs eat better. It was the best of times: you can get education for free. It was the worst of times: but your life paid was the fee.
