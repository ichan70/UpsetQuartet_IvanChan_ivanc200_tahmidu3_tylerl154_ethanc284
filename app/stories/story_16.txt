Once upon a time, there was a princess who lived among the clouds. Her name was Saphire, with long hair that seemed to drag endlessly behind her and eyes so bright they shine like the stars. She was a cheerful child, even turning the upset, rainy clouds into smiley, puffy clouds at her prancing.
