Title: Apocalypse Story Example
Author: UpsetQuartet
Created At: 2026-11-17 23:49:36

The end was neigh. Not nigh: neigh. You might think from Uma Musume or any other horse-related fiction that intelligent horses will be cute and cuddly creates, but no no no, no. They hate everything about racing. They hate humans. And with human intelligence and (somehow) decades of preperation? Human society collapsed.
